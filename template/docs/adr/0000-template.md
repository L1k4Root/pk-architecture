# 0000. Short title of the decision, in the imperative

- Status: proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: who took part

## Context

The forces at play: the problem, constraints, requirements and anything that makes a decision
necessary now. Neutral wording: this is the situation, not the argument.

## Decision

The decision, in full sentences and active voice: "We will …".

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder. Include the costs and the follow-up work. An ADR with no
negative consequences is usually missing something.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — why it was not chosen.
- **Option B** — why it was not chosen.
